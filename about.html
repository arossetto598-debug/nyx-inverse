About the author

Hi, I'm the one holding the pen

Nyx InVerse started as a place to put down the thoughts that show up uninvited — late at night, mid-commute, halfway through washing the dishes. Some arrive as poems. Some insist on being small stories instead. I don't always get a say in which.

This site is that notebook, tidied up just enough to share. If something here sounds like a thought you've also had, that's the whole point of writing it down — tell me about it.
